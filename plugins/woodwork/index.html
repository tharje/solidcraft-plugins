Install Woodwork

  1. Download the zip above (from release v0.4.0).
  2. In SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

Questions and bug reports go to the plugin's author, in the issues on GitHub. If it crashes SolidCraft, harms a design or looks unsafe, please also report it to this list, so others can be warned.

What's new in v0.4.0 (2026-11-23)

Tested on SolidCraft 0.47.0; works on 0.46.

Hinges and drawer slides in the model

Doors show simple concealed hinges and drawers show their slides, and they move with them. Choose the hinge opening (95, 110 or 165 degrees) and the slide type: partial, full extension, over-travel, undermount, or wooden runners with the drawer bottom in a groove. Slides come in standard lengths, and the cut list names them by type and length.

| Before (0.3.0): only the holes | Now: hinges and slides | |---|---| | | |

Wooden runners and undermount slides

Two new drawer slide types. Wooden runners: each drawer's bottom runs in a groove in the cabinet's sides, no hardware. Undermount slides: the slides sit under the drawer box, which is made to fit them. The drawer box board and bottom thickness are Woodwork settings.

*Left: wooden runners. Right: undermount slides. Both cut through the cabinet.*

Joinery in the Cabinet dialog, and see-through boards

Choose how the boards are joined when you make a cabinet: dowels, confirmat screws, plain screws, pocket screws, cam locks, biscuits or Dominos, or a dado. Dividers, fixed shelves and the drawer rail can take a second method. The cut list counts what to buy. Turn on See-through boards and Fastener models in Woodwork settings to look inside. (The separate Joinery function is gone.)

Woodwork settings for the whole design

Joinery, hardware, back, fronts, plinth, labels, cleats, drawer box thickness, cut list and worktop defaults are kept in the design. Apply them to the cabinets you pick, and choose whether the view is framed after a run (Camera).
