Generative Design Engine v0.8.0

Engineering material library (sourced cards), material report, a 2D stress check of flat parts with safety factor, a plate optimiser and a round-bar bracket designer (truss). Third-party plugin, not affiliated with SolidCraft.

Install Generative Design Engine

  1. Download the zip above (from release v0.8.0).
  2. In SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

Questions and bug reports go to the plugin's author, in the issues on GitHub. If it crashes SolidCraft, harms a design or looks unsafe, please also report it to this list, so others can be warned.

What's new in v0.8.0 (2026-11-21)

Third-party plugin, not affiliated with SolidCraft or Tangency Labs; made independently, it only builds on their software. Tested with SolidCraft 0.45.0.

New in 0.8.0: bar-bracket, a round-bar bracket designer (#75, #76)

  • Designs a wall bracket made of round bars and builds it in your design as one body, in one undo step: wall plate, load pad, bars, a ball at every joint, countersunk screw holes and the material.
  • You give the reach, the plate size, the screw holes, the down and sideways loads (sideways is checked both ways), a knock factor, the material, the safety factor, the thinnest bar and the print direction.
  • It tries every bar between a grid of joints (524 candidates by default) and keeps the stiffest layout for all load cases. Then it sizes every bar for strength (counting the weaker print layers) and for buckling.
  • With the default settings it builds 17 bars, 97.2 g, safety factor 11.2 for strength and 4.0 for buckling, and the tip moves 0.6 mm.
  • New SolidCraft permissions: solid.box, solid.cylinder, solid.sphere, solid.hole.

Also in this release (from 0.7.2, not released on its own)

  • Tsai-Wu safety factor for printed parts in the stress3d_printed command-line tool. It gives one number that weighs the stress along and across the print layers together, short and long term (#73).
  • Printed parts stiffer along their layers than across them. Six plastics now carry their across-layer modulus from the data sheet (ASA, PAHT-CF, PC, PA12 MJF, PETG-CF, PLA-CF). The materials entry shows it as across_layers_young_modulus_gpa, and stress3d_printed uses it (or --z-modulus) (#77, #78).

Limits

  • A design aid, not a certified analysis.
