What's new in v0.1.0 (2026-11-13)

First release: Gridfinity bins and baseplates for SolidCraft 0.41 or newer.

Install: download solidcraft-gridfinity-0.1.0.zip below, then in SolidCraft choose Plugins > Install plugin… and pick the zip. The entries appear under Plugins > Gridfinity.

  • Bin: 1 to 6 grid units a side (at most 25 cells), 2 to 20 height units, wall thickness, stacking lip, magnet holes, compartments along X and Y, scoop and label tab.
  • Baseplate: 1 to 6 grid units a side.

Everything is built from ordinary sketches and extrudes, so it stays editable. The plugin needs no files, no network and no clock.

Known limits: no screw holes, no weighted or magnet baseplates, no half-pitch grids. The stacking lip is 4.15 mm tall (a 0.25 mm flat replaces the knife edge). Fit against printed Gridfinity parts has not been test-printed yet.

The .sha256 file checks the download.
