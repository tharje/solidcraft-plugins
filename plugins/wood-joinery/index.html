  1. Download the zip above (from release v0.10.1).

  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

What's new in v0.10.1 (2026-11-21)

Woodworking joints for SolidCraft, as a command plugin. This version fixes nine bugs found by a code review of v0.10.0, and closes the gaps in input checks it pointed out.

Install

You need the SolidCraft desktop application, version 0.42.0 or later. The icons show from version 0.44.0.

  • Download wood-joinery-en.zip (English) or wood-joinery-fr.zip (French) below. Do not take the "Source code" archives, and do not unzip the file.
  • In SolidCraft, open Preferences › Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the file.
  • The commands are in the Plugins group of the Model tab.

Install one package only. Both have the same plugin id. The README has the full steps.

Fixed since v0.10.0

  • Miter with a loose spline: a spline too deep for its thickness could break the outer faces and cut both boards in two. The depth bound now counts the spline's thickness (at most 7.7 mm for a 6 mm spline in 18 mm boards), and the check that no board is left in pieces runs after the slots too.
  • Screws between boards already joined (a tenon, a lap): the first board was moved, or the countersink drilled in the air. They are now refused: screw them before cutting that joint.
  • Half lap of boards of two thicknesses: the thicker board could keep a closed tunnel over its notch. Each notch now runs out through its board's face.
  • Scarf of boards of two thicknesses: both cuts could fall on the same side. Such a scarf is refused: its slope runs from face to face.
  • Align for box joints and miters of boards lying flat: it was refused. The boards now make an L, the end of the first against the edge of the second.
