What's new in v0.50.3 (2026-11-28)

OmiGears 0.50.3 is a rebuild. Every gear is now a component, pairs and sets come joined so that they turn together, and every form has a fit for 3D printing and a choice of bores. It adds whole gearboxes (planetary, compound gear train, cycloidal), timing pulleys, chain sprockets, a ratchet and a Geneva drive. Bevel gears, wrong until now, are right.

Install

  • With OmiGears 0.50.1 or 0.50.2 installed, take the update SolidCraft offers on its card in Preferences › Plugins. Otherwise download omigears-0.50.3.zip below.
  • In SolidCraft (desktop): Preferences › Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip. The review lists the modelling commands it uses, which now include components, placements and joints. It asks for no file or network access.

Requires SolidCraft 0.50.0 or later.

New

  • Every part is a component. A gear is no longer drawn into the design itself: it is a component with one body, drawn about its own origin and put in place by its placement. Move it, copy it or join it as any other component. Equal parts are placements of one component: a planetary set's planets, a gear train's compound gears.
  • Pairs, in mesh and joined. Spur, helical and herringbone gears take Mating gear's teeth; bevel and cycloidal gears build their mate by default; a worm takes its wheel, an internal gear its pinion. Each gear turns on a revolute joint and the joints are coupled with the pair's ratio: drive one and the other follows.
  • Made by: 3D printing. The default fit takes 0.1 mm off each tooth and adds 0.2 mm to each bore, so a printed pair runs and a printed bore takes its shaft. Machining draws the nominal size; Custom shows both numbers. A printed gear's reply says when its teeth are too fine for a 0.4 mm nozzle.
