Gridfinity v1.4.2

Gridfinity bins (hollow, eco or solid), baseplates, and drawer baseplates split to fit your printer or a picked drawer floor, on a full, half or custom grid. Edit bin changes a bin it made, keeping each setting left at Keep.

What it adds to SolidCraft

  • Gridfinity bin
  • Gridfinity baseplate
  • Gridfinity drawer baseplate
  • Edit Gridfinity bin

Install Gridfinity

  1. Download the zip above (from release v1.4.2).
  2. In SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

Questions and bug reports go to the plugin's author, in the issues on GitHub. If it crashes SolidCraft, harms a design or looks unsafe, please also report it to this list, so others can be warned.

What's new in v1.4.2 (2026-11-23)

Needs SolidCraft 0.46.2 or newer. For SolidCraft 0.45, use 1.4.1; for 0.43 or 0.44, 1.3.1.

Screw holes in baseplates (#15, thanks to John Graham)

Baseplates and drawer baseplates have a Screw holes option: an M3 hole through the floor at each magnet spot, countersunk from underneath for a flat-head screw that sits flush. The screw goes up into a bin's screw hole and holds it down. With screw holes the floor is 3.05 mm (M3 × 8), with magnet and screw holes 4.75 mm (M3 × 10). One run builds up to 25 cells with screw holes, 20 with both.

Better joints between drawer pieces

Tabs and sockets now have 0.15 mm clearance and lean in towards the top, so a tab that comes down a little off finds its socket by itself instead of bending its edge. This was needed most on open frames, where the thin socket edges bent and then wouldn't go in at all, and it is now used on every baseplate type.

Label tab width and Auto scoop (#14)

  • Label tab width (5–30 mm, 12 by default), so the shelf fits your label tape. Also in Edit bin.
  • Scoop is now None, Auto or Custom. Auto works the radius out from the compartment: the smallest of the height inside, the depth / 2.5 and 18 mm. Custom uses Scoop radius. Scripts that give a number keep working.

Layout sheet

SolidCraft 0.46.2 starts drawing notes at their point instead of centring them on it, so the numbers and sizes on the drawer's layout sheet sat off-centre and the footer ran off the sheet. They are centred again.
