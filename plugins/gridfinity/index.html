Install Gridfinity

  1. Download the zip above (from release v1.6.0).
  2. In SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

Questions and bug reports go to the plugin's author, in the issues on GitHub. If it crashes SolidCraft, harms a design or looks unsafe, please also report it to this list, so others can be warned.

What's new in v1.6.0 (2026-11-24)

Needs SolidCraft 0.48 or newer. For SolidCraft 0.46.2 or 0.47, use 1.5.0.

The Gridfinity panel

SolidCraft 0.48 lets a plugin dock a panel. Press Panel in the ribbon and Run. It docks at the bottom of the window, with four tabs:

  • Parts: every part the plugin made in the design, with its size, height and label. Click a row to see all its settings.
  • Quick: one click makes a common part beside what is already in the design. The choices are a 1 × 1 × 6 bin, a 2 × 1 × 6 bin with a scoop and a sticker tab, a 2 × 2 × 6 bin in four compartments, an eco bin 3 × 1 × 3 and a 4 × 4 base plate.
  • Layout: click cells away from a base plate in the model (below).
  • Settings: magnet and screw holes, the stacking lip, and hollow or eco bins for the Quick parts. They are kept in the design.

A panel can't yet open the plugin's own forms or tell its buttons which row is picked, so you still change a bin with Edit bin in the ribbon (SolidCraft #21538).

Base plates that aren't rectangles (#2, thanks to kinghat)

Leave out cells on the Base plate form leaves cells empty. Write them as column-row, counted from 1 at the front left, for example 1-3, 2-3, 4-1. Where a cell is left out, the plate ends just as it does at its outer edge.

In the panel's Layout tab, click Remove a cell, then a pocket of a base plate in the model, and the plate is made again without that cell. Each click is one undo step. Restore all cells, then a click on the plate, makes it whole again.
