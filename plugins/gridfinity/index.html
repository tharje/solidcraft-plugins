Gridfinity v1.6.2

Gridfinity bins (hollow, eco or solid), baseplates, and drawer baseplates split to fit your printer or a picked drawer floor, on a full, half or custom grid. Edit bin changes a bin it made, keeping each setting left at Keep.

Install Gridfinity

  1. Download the zip above (from release v1.6.2).
  2. In SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

Questions and bug reports go to the plugin's author, in the issues on GitHub. If it crashes SolidCraft, harms a design or looks unsafe, please also report it to this list, so others can be warned.

What's new in v1.6.2 (2026-11-26)

Needs SolidCraft 0.49.1 or newer; 0.50.0 is best. On 0.49 big bins and plates can hit the time limit, because joins are slow there. For SolidCraft 0.48, use 1.6.1.

Fields in a sensible order (#19, thanks to vilts)

The dialogs no longer list their fields alphabetically. Bin starts with Grid size, Width, Depth and Height, then the inside, dividers, scoop and label. Drawer plate starts with the drawer floor pick and the drawer's size. Custom cell X and Y only show when Grid size is Custom.

Dividers make compartments of the same width (#14, thanks to and-ref)

The stacking lip reaches in over the side walls, so evenly spaced dividers left the outer compartments looking narrower from above. The dividers are now spaced across what the lip leaves open, so every compartment and its label tab looks the same width. At the floor, the outer compartments are a little wider.

Rounded corners where cells are left out (#2, thanks to kinghat)

On a base plate with Leave out cells, the corners the missing cells leave are rounded like the plate's own (4 mm): outer corners are cut round, and inner corners are filled in round. Cells that only meet at a corner become separate bodies. A drawer plate keeps its corners square, to fit its drawer.

For scripts

Base plates and drawer plates no longer have required settings: leave any setting out and it takes its default.
