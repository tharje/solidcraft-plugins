Gridfinity v1.6.1

Gridfinity bins (hollow, eco or solid), baseplates, and drawer baseplates split to fit your printer or a picked drawer floor, on a full, half or custom grid. Edit bin changes a bin it made, keeping each setting left at Keep.

Install Gridfinity

  1. Download the zip above (from release v1.6.1).
  2. In SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

What's new in v1.6.1 (2026-11-24)

Needs SolidCraft 0.48 or newer. For SolidCraft 0.46.2 or 0.47, use 1.5.0.

Magnet hole size (#17, thanks to JoeJambo)

Magnets sold as 6 × 2 mm often come a little bigger or smaller. When you tick Magnet holes, two new fields show:

  • Magnet hole diameter, from 3.5 to 8 mm
  • Magnet hole depth, from 1 to 4 mm

They start at 6.5 × 2.4 mm, as before, so you can measure your magnets and add the clearance you like. They are on bins, base plates and drawer plates, and in Edit bin, where 0 keeps the bin's size.

A deeper hole makes a base plate's floor that much thicker, and an eco bin's hollow feet start higher, over the hole.

For scripts

The new settings are magnet_diameter and magnet_depth on bin, baseplate, drawer and edit.

SolidCraft now checks a script's settings strictly against the Base plate and Drawer plate forms too, as it already did for bins:

  • Give a baseplate its grid, width, depth and bottom.
  • Give a drawer its drawer_width, drawer_depth, printer_x, printer_y, grid and bottom.
  • Use the full choices, such as "42 mm (full)" and "Solid".

Download gridfinity-1.6.1.zip below, then in SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
