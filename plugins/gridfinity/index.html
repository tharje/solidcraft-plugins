Install Gridfinity

  1. Download the zip above (from release v1.7.0).
  2. In SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

It is on the SolidCraft Workshop too: its Open in SolidCraft button installs it, after SolidCraft shows what it asks for.

Questions and bug reports go to the plugin's author, in the issues on GitHub. If it crashes SolidCraft, harms a design or looks unsafe, please also report it to this list, so others can be warned.

What's new in v1.7.0 (2026-11-27)

Needs SolidCraft 0.49.1 or newer; 0.50.0 is best. On 0.49 big bins and plates can hit the time limit, because joins are slow there. For SolidCraft 0.48, use 1.6.1.

Lids for bins

Bin has a new Lid setting: None, Flat or Stackable. The lid is made beside the bin, ready to print without supports.

  • Its plug is shaped like the inside of the stacking lip, so the lid sits in the lip and stays on by friction. Its plate rests on top of the lip.
  • Lid fit sets the room between plug and lip on each side: 0.15 mm by default, where a stacked bin has 0.25. Less holds tighter, more comes off easier.
  • A flat lid lies plate down, with a hollow plug on top (a 2×1 lid takes 9.3 cm³).
  • A stackable lid has a stacking lip on top, so another bin stands on it. It stands on its plug, which is solid so it prints without supports (a 3×2 lid takes 63.6 cm³).
  • A lid needs the bin's stacking lip.

Checked in SolidCraft, not yet printed: please report how it fits, and which Lid fit works on your printer.

Edit bin shows the bin's settings (#20, thanks to Rob65)

When you pick a bin in Edit bin, every field now fills in with that bin's own settings: width, depth, height, dividers, scoop, label tab and text, magnets, screw holes and stacking lip. Change only what you want, then Apply. A field you've changed yourself is not filled in again.

For scripts

  • bin takes lid ("None" / "Flat" / "Stackable") and lid_fit (0–0.5 mm, 0.15).
  • edit works as before: a field left out, or at Keep/0, keeps the bin's own value.
