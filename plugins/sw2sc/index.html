Install SolidWorks Parts (sw2sc)

  1. Download the zip above (from release v0.17.3).
  2. In SolidCraft: Plugins › Install plugin… and pick the zip.
  3. Run it from the Plugins menu, or its button in the ribbon (SolidCraft 0.42 and later).

Questions and bug reports go to the plugin's author, in the issues on GitHub. If it crashes SolidCraft, harms a design or looks unsafe, please also report it to this list, so others can be warned.

What's new in v0.17.3 (2026-11-23)

A sketch's chain record is read, and a rebuild that doesn't match now says what shape its cause has.

Added

  • A sketch's chain record is read and kept. moSketchChain_c — 196 parts write one, at the end of the sketch — is a u16 count and then that many u32 point ids: the points a chain runs through, in order, in the sketch's own numbering (Bucket's 12 are its 12 lines' points, spring_holder_v1's 10, PartT_7's 18, 19, 20, 17). Kept on Sketch.chain. It does not settle F-047: the points the chains name but the reader lacks have no record of their own anywhere in the sketch.
  • A rebuild that doesn't match is signed by the shape of its cause. verify.mismatch_signature names the first thing to look at: too much material points at a loop taken as an island or a cut that didn't take, too little at a missing feature or a wrong direction, the same volume with a different area at a missing or wrong fillet or chamfer, and a box that moved on its own at the placement.

Added (plugin)

  • plugin/: a SolidCraft importer plugin (Rust, WebAssembly) that opens .sldprt files with their feature tree, checked against the part's own solid through SolidCraft's evaluate interface. A port of this converter: the same reading on all 199 sample parts, the same designs, the same rebuilds on the 22 compared.

Fixed

  • Opening a large pre-2015 file could take minutes. Finding where its numbering starts numbered the whole feature stream for each of up to 600 starts; only the stream up to where moRefPlane_c is brought in decides it now (the same start on all 33 old samples, which read in 3.3 s together).
