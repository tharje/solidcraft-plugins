What's new in v0.3.0 (2026-11-19)

All six plugins now share internal part definitions and verified component reuse. Bearings and V-wheels add supplier products with visible exterior detail. The Bearing dialog also offers a supplier-neutral sealed 105. CSK clutches add customizable exterior geometry with reference sources recorded separately.

Bearings and V-wheels

  • Bearings offers base size and closure or a search by nominal bore, outside diameter and width. Thirteen closed variants use estimated exteriors. Twelve carry catalog-confirmed MISUMI numbers, and the sealed 105 has a supplier-neutral BOM row. Open remains available for 626. The product table lists each exterior basis.
  • V-wheels offers Solid, Xtreme Solid, grooved Delrin, Delrin Mini and Xtreme Mini products. Choose a bare wheel or a core comprising one wheel, two bearings and one internal shim.
  • Direct bearings and wheel assemblies reuse the same verified bearing definition. The wheel package contains its dependencies; installing Bearings separately is optional.
  • Core assemblies list their separate purchases in the BOM. They are not retail kits and do not include mounting axles, external spacers, washers or nuts.
  • Geometry is built from native features using measured STEP profiles. Source hashes, dimensional discrepancies and the 105ZZ correction from 4.2 to 4 mm are documented. Original STEP files are not distributed.

Bearing closures are intentional zero-thickness surface bodies; rings are exterior envelopes. These models support layout and appearance, not physical bearing mass, internal contact or manufacturing sections. Metal wheels and the supplied inch and 8 × 16 × 5 bearings remain unpublished candidates pending exact product evidence.
